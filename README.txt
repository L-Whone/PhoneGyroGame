PhoneGryoGame
https://github.com/L-Whone/PhoneGyroGame.git


How To Play:
- Download unity Project and Open
- Add Build Profile for IOS/Android (whichever you want to use) to project
- Go to Project Settings -> Editor -> Device to Any IOS Device/AndroidDevice
- Download Unity Remote 6 on your device
- Connect your phone to your PC
- IF on IOS download iTunes and connect iPhone
- Open Menu scene in Unity Project -> Assets -> Scenes
- Press Play and play on the phone

ADDED: 
- Swipe Input to add force to ball
- The further you swipe, the higher the force
